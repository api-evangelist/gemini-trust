# Gemini Trust Company

Gemini Trust Company, LLC is a United States cryptocurrency exchange and custodian, founded in 2014 and headquartered in New York, regulated as a New York trust company. It operates the Gemini Exchange, offering REST and WebSocket APIs for market data, order management, account balances, clearing, earn/staking and institutional capital accounts.
